01

Rebuilding Secondary Sales for a National FMCG Distributor

How a national FMCG distributor moved from end-of-day paper reconciliation to live secondary-sales visibility across 320 reps in six weeks — and lifted productivity 5X.

Client
National FMCG Distributor
Pan-India distributor covering 14,000+ retail outlets across 9 states. Anonymous per NDA.

01 The Problem

  • Sales reps were spending 40-60 minutes a day on paper order books and end-of-day data entry. Productive selling hours were eaten by admin.
  • Once inventory left the distributor warehouse, the brand had no visibility into what actually moved at retail. Stockouts at outlets coexisted with overstock at distributors.
  • Beat plans were drawn 6 months prior and no longer matched reality. Reps cherry-picked easy outlets. Coverage degraded silently. Quarterly targets were missed three quarters in a row.

02 The Solution

  • Deployed Infooware's Field Sales Intelligence platform — order booking mobile app, beat planning engine, distributor dashboard, AI Sales Copilot.
  • Onboarded 320 field reps over six weeks. In-person training in 4 regional hubs. Offline-first mobile app meant no signal-dependency in rural beats.
  • Integrated with the client's existing SAP ERP and Tally accounting for downstream order processing and reconciliation.

03 Architecture

  • Mobile app: React Native (offline-first), local SQLite cache, syncs on signal
  • Backend: Node.js microservices on AWS ECS · Postgres for transactional, Redshift for analytics
  • Real-time tracking: WebSocket layer, MQTT-based location pings
  • AI Copilot: RAG over product catalog + historical orders, served via OpenAI + custom retrieval
  • Integrations: SAP IDoc connector, Tally TCP bridge, WhatsApp Business API for receipts

02

Unifying EHR Across a 14-Location Multi-Specialty Hospital Chain

Consolidated patient records, lab integration and compliance reporting across 14 hospital locations — audit-ready in 4 months, with 40% faster record retrieval.

Client
Multi-Specialty Hospital Chain
Mid-sized hospital chain operating 14 facilities across western and southern India. Anonymous per NDA.

01 The Problem

  • Patient records were fragmented across 14 facilities, each running a different EMR vendor. A patient referred from one hospital to another arrived with no usable record continuity.
  • Lab and pharmacy systems were standalone — clinicians manually copied results between systems, with a daily reconciliation team chasing mismatches.
  • NABH and HIPAA audits were months-long fire drills. The compliance team spent more time preparing audit packages than running the program.
  • OPD throughput suffered: average wait time at registration was 22 minutes; billing took another 18 minutes post-consult.

02 The Solution

  • Deployed Infooware's Healthcare Platform (ERP) with unified EHR, integrated billing, pharmacy and lab modules, plus mobile patient and doctor apps.
  • Phased rollout: pilot at flagship facility (month 1-2), expansion to 4 additional sites (month 3-4), rollout to remaining 9 (month 5-7).
  • Built HL7/FHIR connectors to migrate 1.2M existing patient records. Lab and pharmacy systems integrated via DICOM and proprietary middleware adapters.
  • Compliance dashboard tracks HIPAA, NABH and ABDM requirements continuously — flagging gaps before audit cycles.

03 Architecture

  • Frontend: React (web), React Native (patient/doctor apps)
  • Backend: Node.js + GraphQL gateway, Postgres for transactional, MongoDB for clinical notes
  • FHIR-compliant data layer, HL7 message bus (RabbitMQ)
  • Hosting: Private cloud on AWS Mumbai · HIPAA-aligned VPC isolation
  • AI: Symptom-driven differential diagnosis (Knowledge Agent), drug interaction alerts, no-show predictor

03

Farmer Marketplace & Traceability Platform for 8,000+ Smallholders

Built an end-to-end marketplace connecting 8,000+ smallholder farmers in Kenya and Tanzania to bulk buyers, with crop-level traceability and carbon tracking baked in.

Client
East African Agri Cooperative
Multi-country agri cooperative federating 8,000+ smallholder farmers across Kenya and Tanzania. Anonymous per NDA.

01 The Problem

  • Smallholder farmers were selling crops at farmgate prices to local aggregators, capturing only 20-30% of the eventual buyer price. The value chain bled their margin.
  • Bulk buyers (food processors, exporters, supermarket chains) couldn't reliably source from smallholders — no traceability, no quality grading, no aggregation logistics.
  • EU and US buyers were demanding carbon-footprint data for sourcing decisions. The cooperative had no way to capture or report this.
  • Existing FPO software was Western-built, English-only, and assumed always-on connectivity that didn't match rural East African reality.

02 The Solution

  • Deployed Infooware's Agrosphere Smart Agriculture Platform + custom marketplace layer with Swahili and English UI, offline-first mobile architecture.
  • GIS-mapped 8,000+ farm plots. Crop intelligence module advised planting and harvest windows by location. Traceability captured every batch from sowing to dispatch.
  • Built carbon tracking module — soil carbon, fertilizer use, transport emissions — generating buyer-ready reports.
  • Aggregation logistics layer coordinates pickup from farms, quality grading at hubs, and dispatch to buyers. Payments flow back to farmers via M-Pesa.

03 Architecture

  • Mobile: Flutter (offline-first, Swahili + English), works on entry-level Android
  • Backend: GraphQL + Node.js on Google Cloud Africa region
  • GIS: PostGIS + Mapbox · Satellite imagery via Sentinel-2
  • Payments: M-Pesa Daraja API, Flutterwave for cross-border
  • AI: Yield forecasting (per-farm), pest/disease early warning (image classification)

04

Digital Inspection Platform Replacing Paper Audits Across 47 Sites

Replaced paper-based safety, quality and compliance audits across 47 manufacturing sites in 6 countries with a unified inspection platform — and shaved audit cycle time by 75%.

Client
Industrial Manufacturing Group
Multi-country industrial group operating 47 production facilities across India, Vietnam, Thailand, Mexico, Poland and Brazil. Anonymous per NDA.

01 The Problem

  • Quality, safety and compliance audits were 100% paper-based across 47 sites. Each site had its own checklists, scoring rubrics and reporting cadences.
  • Auditors were re-keying the same data into 3 different systems: site QA tool, central compliance system, and the regulator-facing portal.
  • Findings took 11-14 days to surface to corporate leadership. Corrective actions had no closed-loop tracking — half went unverified.
  • Annual external audits required teams to physically reconstruct trails from disparate paper records. Two near-misses with regulators in the past three years.

02 The Solution

  • Deployed Infooware's TrackSure Smart Inspection & Certification platform with Audit & Compliance Mobile App for on-site auditors.
  • Standardized 200+ inspection checklists across 47 sites with country-specific compliance overlays. Auditors capture findings on tablet/phone, photos timestamped and geotagged.
  • Corrective Action workflows close the loop automatically — assigned, tracked, verified, signed off with full audit trail.
  • Compliance dashboards roll up by site, country, region and global — surfacing trends, recurring failures, high-risk facilities.

03 Architecture

  • Mobile: React Native (offline-first, multi-language)
  • Backend: .NET Core microservices on Azure (regional deployment for data residency)
  • Document management: Azure Blob with WORM compliance
  • Reporting: PowerBI embedded · Custom regulator-facing report templates
  • AI: Audit Copilot suggests next-question logic, flags inconsistent answers, drafts CAPA tickets

05

Role-Based ERP for a Multi-Campus Education Group

Unified 7 campuses, 32,000 students, 1,800 staff and tens of thousands of parents onto a single role-based platform — with AI-powered learning paths and auto-evaluation built in.

Client
Multi-Campus Education Group
K-12 + higher education group operating 7 campuses across western India. Anonymous per NDA.

01 The Problem

  • Each of the 7 campuses ran its own SIS, LMS, fee management and parent communication tools — 28+ disconnected systems in total.
  • Parents received SMS, email, WhatsApp and printed letters from different systems. Important academic notifications were missed routinely.
  • Examination evaluation was a manual marathon. Teachers spent weekends grading. Result publication lagged 3-4 weeks after exams.
  • Fee collection had a 13% leakage. No central visibility on who paid, who was due, who needed follow-up.

02 The Solution

  • Deployed Infooware's EduCloud ERP with role-based dashboards (Admin / Teacher / Student / Parent) plus the AI Learning Platform for personalized learning paths.
  • Migrated 32,000 student records, 1,800 staff records, and 5 years of academic history. Parallel-ran with legacy systems for one academic term to validate.
  • AI auto-evaluation handles MCQ, short-answer and even structured long-form responses. Teacher review time on assessments dropped 70%.
  • Single parent app replaces fragmented communications. Attendance, fee status, academic progress, exam results — all in one place.

03 Architecture

  • Frontend: Next.js (web), React Native (mobile)
  • Backend: Node.js + tRPC, Postgres, Redis for session state
  • AI: GPT-4 for evaluation, custom RAG for subject-specific knowledge
  • Payments: Razorpay integration · Auto-reconciliation against fee schedules
  • Notifications: WhatsApp Business + SMS + email, unified preferences per parent

06

AI-Powered Operations for a Boutique Hotel Chain

Centralized booking, dynamic pricing, housekeeping and POS for a 23-property boutique hotel chain across 4 countries — and grew occupancy 2X without growing room inventory.

Client
Boutique Hotel Group
Boutique hospitality group operating 23 properties across India, Sri Lanka, Vietnam and Indonesia. Anonymous per NDA.

01 The Problem

  • Properties used different PMS vendors. The group had no real-time visibility into occupancy, RevPAR or housekeeping status across the portfolio.
  • Pricing was set monthly by property managers. Dynamic demand swings (festivals, conferences, weather events) were missed — leaving revenue on the table.
  • Guest preferences captured at one property didn't follow to others. A guest's repeat visit started cold every time.
  • F&B, spa and front-desk POS were siloed. End-of-day reconciliation across systems was a 90-minute manual exercise per property.

02 The Solution

  • Deployed Hotelligence — AI-Powered Hotel Ops across all 23 properties, with central dashboard and per-property operational views.
  • Dynamic pricing engine ingests competitor rates, local event calendars, historical demand patterns and current pipeline — recommends rate adjustments daily.
  • Guest CRM follows the guest across properties. Stay preferences, F&B history, allergies, anniversary dates — all surfaced at check-in.
  • Unified POS for front-desk, F&B, spa and ancillary services. End-of-day close went from 90 minutes to under 10.

03 Architecture

  • Frontend: React (operations dashboards), React Native (housekeeping tablets)
  • Backend: Node.js + GraphQL, multi-tenant Postgres
  • AI Pricing: Custom forecasting model + LLM rationale generator
  • Integrations: Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda channel manager · Stripe + local PSPs
  • PMS: Built-in, with optional Opera / Cloudbeds bridge for legacy properties
