Our Process

Seven phases. Every project.

PHASE 01 · WEEK 1-2

Discovery

Stakeholder interviews. Product audit. Success metrics defined. Technical feasibility validated. Architecture sketched.

PHASE 02 · WEEK 2-3

Sprint Planning

Backlog shaped. Milestones defined. Team loaded. Risk register opened. First sprint goals locked.

PHASE 03 · WEEK 2-6

UX Design

Information architecture. Wireframes. Interactive prototypes. Design system. User testing rounds.

PHASE 04 · WEEK 4+

Agile Development

Two-week sprints. Daily standups. End-of-sprint demos. Code reviews. Continuous integration from day one.

PHASE 05 · ONGOING

Quality Assurance

Test automation. Performance benchmarks. Security audits. Accessibility compliance. UAT coordination.

PHASE 06 · GO-LIVE

Deployment

Staged rollout. Blue-green strategy. Production observability. Incident playbooks. Day-one support.

PHASE 07 · POST-LAUNCH

Continuous Improvement

Telemetry-driven iteration. Quarterly business reviews. Roadmap refinement. Feature velocity sustained.

Engagement Models

Four ways to engage us.

Match the engagement to the work — defined scope, evolving scope, team build-out, or shared upside.

MODEL · 01

Fixed Cost

You bring a clear scope, deliverables and timeline. We give you a fixed quote and ship to it. Best for MVPs, well-defined modernization projects, and time-boxed engagements.

  • Best for: defined scope
  • Timeline: 4-16 weeks typical
  • Risk profile: predictable
MODEL · 02

Dedicated Team

A handpicked pod — engineers, designers, QA, PM — operates as an extension of your team. Best for evolving products, long-term roadmaps, and when scope will breathe.

  • Best for: long-term build-out
  • Pod size: 3-15 people
  • Commitment: 3-month minimum
MODEL · 03

Build · Operate · Transfer (BOT)

We stand up the capability, hire the team, run operations, then transfer everything — people, processes, IP — to your in-house ownership.

  • Best for: setting up a tech center
  • Timeline: 12-24 months
  • Outcome: fully transferred capability
MODEL · 04

Product Partnership

Co-investment where we share upside via revenue sharing or equity. Best when the product thesis is strong, market timing matters, and capital is constrained.

  • Best for: early-stage co-build
  • Structure: revenue or equity share
  • Selective & mutually agreed
