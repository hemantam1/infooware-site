Our Story

From services to a platforms studio.

We started in Ahmedabad with a small team and a stubborn belief: that good software, shipped consistently, compounds into something rare — trust. Fifteen years later, that trust has carried us to over a hundred global clients and the better part of five hundred live projects.

Along the way, certain patterns kept recurring: field sales teams losing their day to paper; hospitals chasing patient records across silos; FMCG distributors flying blind on secondary sales. Each became a product. Today we run 19 of them — and we use what we learn from running them to make our services work better.

This is the unfair advantage we bring to every engagement: we don't just build software. We've operated it. At scale. In the markets you care about.

Our Values

Four operating principles.

01

Ship — then iterate

Production beats slide decks. Working software is the only honest signal. We aim for shipping in every sprint, and improving in every release.

02

Operate, don't just build

We run our own platforms in production. That bias shows up in your code: better observability, saner deployments, fewer 3 AM pages.

03

Domain depth over framework breadth

We pick a sector and go deep — agriculture, healthcare, FMCG, retail. Frameworks change. Domain intelligence compounds.

04

Make the simple thing first

Complexity is a tax. The best architectures we ship are surprisingly small. Add only when reality demands it.
